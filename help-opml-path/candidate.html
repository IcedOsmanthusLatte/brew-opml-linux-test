Brew Help

How can we help?

A few answers to help you find your sources, settle into reading, and get more from Brew.

Getting started

What is Brew?
Brew is a simple, privacy-first RSS reader for iOS that helps you discover, subscribe to, and read the content you love in one place. Choose your own sources, discover and subscribe to curated lists shared by other users in Discovery, or share lists of your own.
Is Brew free?
Yes. Brew is free to use, and a free version will remain available long term. If we introduce optional paid features in the future, we’ll share the details in advance.
Which devices does Brew support?
Brew is currently available for iPhone with iOS 17 or later, with the beta distributed through TestFlight. Plans for Android and other platforms have not been announced.

Feeds & newsletters

Does Brew support newsletters?
Yes. Subscribe to newsletters using the dedicated email address generated for you in Brew, then receive and read them together in the app.
Can I import and export OPML files?
OPML import and export are supported, making it easier to bring subscriptions over from another reader or take them with you. If some sources fail to import, send us the OPML file and details of the affected sources so we can investigate. See the OPML import steps for iPhone.
Can I add an RSS feed manually?
Yes. Paste a valid RSS or Atom feed URL to add a subscription.
Why do some feeds fail to refresh or show incomplete content?
Possible causes include broken feed URLs, website access restrictions, fetching failures, or content formatting issues. First check that the feed URL is valid. If the issue continues, send us the URL, the time it happened, and an error screenshot so we can investigate.

Reading & understanding

Can I browse Threads in Brew?
Yes. You can browse Threads content within the Brew app.
Does Brew offer AI summaries and translation?
Yes. Brew supports AI article summaries and translation to help you get the main points and read content in other languages. AI summaries may miss details or contain errors, so check important information against the original. Morning and evening briefings, news catch-up delivery, and duplicate content detection are not available yet.
Does Brew support full-text reading, ad removal, and embedded video?
Yes. Brew offers full-text reading and ad removal to help you focus on the content. It also supports embedded video playback, reducing the need to switch between apps.

Customization & what’s next

Can I open Brew from an external launcher?
We’ve settled on Universal Links, using https://open.getbrew.app with support for a trailing slash. The approach is confirmed; details of supported app versions and launcher compatibility will be shared in future release notes.
Can I customize card image cropping and display styles?
A full set of customization options is not available yet. We’ve noted the request and will share details of any features and timing in future release notes.
Can I hide the time and status bar while reading?
Brew currently keeps the existing blur effect. We plan to add a status bar visibility setting so you can choose whether to hide it. A release date has not been announced.
Does Brew support home screen widgets and read-later organization?
Home screen widgets and more ways to organize articles for later have been noted as future requests. No release date has been announced.

Privacy & support

How does Brew protect my reading privacy?
Privacy is a focus of Brew’s design. You can browse currently supported public content from services such as Reddit and YouTube without signing in to those platforms. For details of data collection, storage, and third-party services, please see Brew’s Privacy Policy.
How can I report a bug or suggest a feature?
Use the feedback option in Brew, email support@getbrew.app, or contact the team in the Discord community. For bugs, include your app version, device and iOS version, steps to reproduce the issue, and screenshots. For subscription issues, include the feed URL as well.

Still need help?

Questions, feedback, or a bug to report? Email us at support@getbrew.app.
